# Project Retrospective  

## What went well  

When I started this project, I knew what I wanted to do but not exactly how to execute it. I like the way it ended up being formatted, the color scheme, and the images of the animals. With this project, I did not have any issues with duplicate documents or missing documents like I have before.  

## What did not go well  

I had trouble getting the images to show up on the link. It took me a while and some research to figure it out. Once I finally figured out how to add the images, I had to figure out how to get them to not interrupt the paragraphs.  

## What I would do differently next time  

I would like to be able to find out how to add in more elements and designs. Perhaps a nicer logo or heading would have looked better.
