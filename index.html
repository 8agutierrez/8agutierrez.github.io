Fierce Felines

Fun Facts about Feline Animals

The Cheetah

The cheetah is known as the fastest land animal alive as it can reach 112 km/hr in 3 seconds! Cheetahs are social animals and are usually found in groups of mothers with cubs or siblings. Adult females are more solitary and typically only get together to mate.

The Lion

African lions live in groups called Prides. Male lions are easy to distingush from females because they have a prominent maine. A male lions mane is a symbol of their fighting ability and health to both potential mates, and rivals.
